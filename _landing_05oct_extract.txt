4) BUILT FOR REGULATED ENVIRONMENTS
Intelligence Without Giving Up Control.
Suricat fits into existing quality and regulatory environments while keeping QA and RA professionals in control.
Human AccountabilitySuricat identifies potential compliance issues and links each one to its source evidence. QA and RA professionals review each potential issue, verify the evidence, and decide what to do. Responsibility for every decision remains with them.
No Rip and ReplaceExisting QMS and document control systems stay in place. Suricat works alongside those systems and established processes.
Read Only by DesignSuricat evaluates compliance information without modifying controlled documents or source records. Existing approval processes and document controls remain intact.
Data OwnershipDocuments, records, and compliance information remain the customer's property. Customer data is not shared with other customers or used to train AI models without explicit written consent.
Dedicated InstanceEach customer receives a dedicated Suricat instance, keeping compliance information isolated from other organizations.
